## Known open items carried into later passes
- Proctor Odd's Bean/Power transfer wording (#48) still needs clearer rules text.
- Team names: only icons exist, no names — cosmetic/UX item for print, not a balance issue.
- Captain Crunch (#7) printed card text is now the ONLY card in the deck still showing pre-redesign wording — the reprinted PDF updated its Bean cost to -1 (matching the nerf) but not its ability text, which still reads "Choose an opponent's board: move this card and another card of your choice to it" rather than the current follow-the-redirected-card behavior. Needs a final reprint pass.

## Resolved
- **Ability resolution timing**: confirmed by designer — abilities resolve after each round (not bundled at the end of Round 3). Rulebook's Combat section has been corrected to add this as step 4 of each round.
- **Port Melanin's missing team icon**: confirmed intentional — this card is deliberately teamless, not a printing gap.
- **Flip mechanic "inconsistency"**: not actually inconsistent — Handfoot's flip swaps Bean/Power (3/2 → 2/3, confirmed by designer); Craig 8's flip gives a wholly new stat line (2/2 → 4/0). Two valid flavors of the same keyword, no fix needed.
- **Gym Shark / James Madison rate comparison**: designer confirmed fine as-is, no change.
- **Team size range (4-7 per team)**: designer confirmed fine as-is, no change.
- **Board/Column terminology**: each player has their own Board; each round creates its own Column on it (3-column array after Round 3). Moved cards land in the target's current-round Column and count toward that player's total. Rulebook now defines this explicitly.
- **Prepare phase**: confirmed all four bullets happen together every Prepare turn — rulebook updated with a "Do all of the following" lead-in.
- **Multi-player wound scope**: confirmed only the lowest-Power player(s) get wounded; everyone else is unaffected — rulebook now states this explicitly.
- **Unopposed-challenge Trophy value**: keeping at 2 Trophies for now, no change.
- **Deck size / duplicate-Hero rules**: confirmed 108 cards (two copies of each of the 54 designs).
- **Displeased Avian targeting**: confirmed own-board-only, defensive (ejects your own worst card).
- **Voodude and Captain Crunch redesigned**: both simulated as extreme outliers (74.8% and 7.9-9.5% combat win rate) and have been reworked — Voodude now destroys the strongest card in play and moves into its spot; Captain Crunch now redistributes the strongest opposing threat to the weakest opponent. Both normalized to the ~31-40% range after redesign.
- **Brain Freeze artwork**: confirmed fixed in the reprinted PDF — full illustration now present, no longer blank.
- **Grup Scrooge's "opponent's area" wording**: confirmed fixed in the reprinted PDF — now reads "opponent's board," matching the standardized terminology.
- **Debit's "extra Beans" ambiguity**: confirmed fixed in the reprinted PDF — reworded to "leftover Beans," which reads unambiguously as unspent Beans at that point.
- **Voodude's printed text**: reprinted PDF's ability text now explicitly matches our destroy-and-reposition redesign (confirms the design was correct), and also reduced Voodude's Power 2→1 in the same pass.
