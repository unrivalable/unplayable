01

Goal

Be the first player to earn 3 Trophies.

Trophies come from winning Combats — either by having the highest total Power when a Combat is contested, or by challenging when nobody dares to fight you.

02

Setup

  1. Shuffle all Hero Cards into a single deck.
  2. Deal 7 cards to each player.
  3. Place 4 cards face up beside the deck to create the Trader.
  4. Place 1 card face up beside the deck to begin the Discard Pile.
  5. The tallest player takes the first turn.
Running out of cards
If the Hero Deck runs out at any time, shuffle the Discard Pile to create a new Hero Deck.
03

Your Turn: Prepare or Challenge

Unplayable is played in two phases — Preparation and Combat. On your turn, choose one action: Prepare, or Challenge.

Prepare

Do all of the following:

  • Draw 2 cards from the Deck, the Trader, the Discard Pile, or any combination of them.
  • If your hand contains duplicate Heroes, you may discard any duplicate and immediately draw a replacement — repeat as many times as you wish.
  • Discard 2 cards from your hand.
  • Refill the Trader back to 4 cards if needed.

Challenge

Instead of preparing, shout “JUICE IT UP!” This immediately starts Combat.

04

Joining a Combat

When Combat begins, every other player secretly decides whether to join.

After counting down 3… 2… 1…, everyone reveals at once:

👍 Thumbs Up

Join the Combat.

👎 Thumbs Down

Stay out. Players who stay out cannot join later.

Unopposed Challenge
If nobody joins, the challenger immediately gains 2 Trophies, and the game returns to the Preparation Phase.
05

Combat

Combat lasts 3 rounds. A player's Board is the area in front of them containing all Heroes they've played during the current Combat.

Each round

  1. Secretly play 0–3 Hero Cards face down.
  2. Reveal all cards at the same time.
  3. Resolve any Hero Abilities that trigger when revealed.
  4. Place the revealed Heroes into that round's column on your Board.
Duplicate Heroes
You cannot play duplicate Heroes during the same Combat.

Some Hero Abilities may allow you to play more than 3 Heroes during a round.

After Round 3

  1. Resolve any remaining Hero Abilities.
  2. Apply Team Bonuses (see below).
  3. Add together the total Power of all Heroes.

The player(s) with the highest total Power win the Combat.

06

Hero Cards

Every Hero Card includes a Name, Power, Bean Value, Team, and an Ability.

1
Bean Value
Top-left of the ability box. Positive values generate Beans when played; negative values cost Beans to play.
2
Power
Top-right of the ability box. Added to your total at the end of Combat.
3
Ability
Printed in the center box — may trigger on a specific round, on any play, or as part of a Combo.
4
Team
Shown by the icon at the bottom of the card — relevant for the Team Bonus.

Beans

  • Heroes with a positive Bean Value generate Beans when played.
  • Heroes with a negative Bean Value cost Beans to play.
  • You may only play a Hero with a negative Bean Value if you've generated enough Beans during previous rounds of the current Combat or during the current round to pay its cost.
  • When Heroes are revealed, first generate all positive Beans, then pay the costs of all negative Bean Heroes.

Hero Abilities

Unless a Hero says otherwise, its Ability resolves immediately when it is revealed.

07

Combo Abilities

Some Heroes list one or more Combo Heroes. A Combo activates only if all required Heroes are played during the same round.

+

Snow Angel and Captain Christingle form a Combo — play them both in the same round and each triggers its bonus.

08

Team Bonus

If you play 2 or more Heroes from the same Team during a Combat, each Hero from that Team gains +1 Power at the end of Combat.

09

End of Combat & Wounds

Each player tied for the highest total Power gains 1 Trophy.

After Combat

  • Discard all Heroes that were played.
  • Keep all unused cards in your hand.
  • Draw until you have 7 cards.
  • Refill the Trader back to 4 cards.
Wounds
Every player tied for the lowest total Power receives 1 Wound.

A player with a Wound may only draw back up to 6 cards during their next Preparation Phase. After that turn, the Wound is removed.

10

Winning

The first player to earn 3 Trophies wins the game.

Good luck… and JUICE IT UP!

11

Quick Combat Summary

  1. Players Prepare until someone shouts “JUICE IT UP!”
  2. Players secretly choose whether to join the Combat.
  3. Play 3 rounds of Heroes.
  4. Resolve Hero Abilities.
  5. Apply Team Bonuses.
  6. Compare total Power.
  7. Award Trophies and Wounds.
  8. Discard played Heroes.
  9. Refill the Trader.
  10. Draw back up to your maximum hand size.
12

Expansion: Location Cards

Oh, the Places You'll Go!

This expansion adds Location Cards — a separate deck that changes the rules of each Combat, one round at a time.

Setup

Shuffle the Location Deck separately from the Hero Deck. At the beginning of every Preparation Phase, before the first player's turn, reveal 3 Location Cards face up in the center of the play area, arranged left to right as:

Round 1 Location
Round 2 Location
Round 3 Location

Each Location affects only its matching Combat round.

Location Cards have no Bean, Power, or Team — just a Name and an effect that applies to whichever round it's assigned to.

During Combat

Before each round begins, apply that round's matching Location effect. Location effects apply to every player participating in that round, unless the Location says otherwise, and remain active for the entire round unless another card changes them.

End of Combat

Discard all three Location Cards. At the beginning of the next Preparation Phase, reveal 3 new Location Cards.

Expansion Hero — Clamfish
Ability: Replace this round's Location. When Clamfish is revealed, discard the current Location for that round and immediately reveal a new Location from the top of the Location Deck. The new Location takes effect immediately.
